    How it decides

    The rules every trade has to pass.

    1. Proven bettors only. A wallet counts if it is in the top 100 of Polymarket's sports leaderboard this month and the top 200 all-time.
    2. Agreement. At least 2 of those wallets hold the same side. A wallet holding both sides as a hedge doesn't count.
    3. Little pushback. Vetted money on the other side is under 10% of the money on the pick.
    4. Fair price. Kalshi's price is within 3¢ of what the leaders paid, on the same line.
    5. Enter in the last hour. Each hourly run enters games starting — minutes out: one trade per game, the passing market with the most leader money.
    6. 15-minute check. A check runs every 15 minutes. In a game's last 20 minutes before the start, its open trade must still have 2 or more vetted wallets on the pick and opposition under 10%, or it is sold.
    7. Leave with the leaders. Hourly runs also sell a trade if most of its leaders sell or flip before the start; otherwise it is held to settlement.
